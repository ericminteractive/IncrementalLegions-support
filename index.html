Privacy Policy

Incremental Legions · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Short version: the whole game can be played offline. When you're online, the game backs up your progress to its server and ranks your best stage and tower floors. Your account is anonymous unless you choose Sign in with Apple, and even then the game does not ask Apple for your name or email address. Your chosen name and your places on the rankings are public. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (your heroes, items, relics, research, castle, and currencies, the stages you have cleared, and your settings) is saved on your device. Anything waiting to be sent to the server is kept there too until you are online.

Your account

  • The first time the game goes online, it makes an anonymous account for you. It is kept on this device.
  • With Sign in with Apple (in Settings), Apple gives the game an identifier that is unique to this developer. The game does not ask Apple for your name or your email address. Your progress can then follow your account to another device.

What is sent to the game's server, and why

  • Cloud save: a copy of your progress and a short summary of it (such as your stage and your currencies), so you can restore it after reinstalling the game or on another device. It comes with a random identifier made for this install of the game, which only tells a save made here from one made elsewhere.
  • Your bests: the highest stage you have cleared and your highest floor in each element tower, and when you reached them, for the rankings.
  • Your name: the name you choose for the rankings. A filter keeps out offensive names.
  • Purchase records: the signed transaction information Apple provides (the product, the date, and a transaction number), so the server can confirm a purchase. If Apple refunds a purchase, Apple tells the server.
  • The game's version: so the server can tell you when an update is needed. The server also gives the game its clock, for daily resets, events, and Hot Time.

With no internet connection, the game keeps playing, and nothing is sent until you are online again.

What other players can see

  • Your name.
  • Your places on the rankings: the world ranking (your highest stage) and the element towers' rankings (your highest floors).

There is no chat and no messaging, so no player can send you anything.

What is never collected

Your real name, your email address, your contacts, your location, your photos, and your advertising identifier. Nothing the game sends can recognize you in any other app.

Reports and blocking

You can report a player's name from the rankings. A report sends the player you reported, the reason, and your account's identifier to the developer, who reads it and can rename a player or remove them from the rankings. Blocking a player hides them from you.

Where it is stored

The game's server is run for the developer by Supabase, Inc. (the database, accounts, and server functions), which processes the data only for this purpose. Sign in with Apple and purchases are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

How long it is kept

Your account and what belongs to it stay until you delete the account.

Deleting your account

Use "Delete account" in Settings. It removes your account and everything under it from the server: your cloud save, your name, your places on the rankings, your purchase records, and your blocks and reports. If you signed in with Apple, the game also asks Apple to end its access. Your progress on the device stays until you delete the game.

In-app purchases

Purchases are optional and are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your card number or your billing details, only the transaction information Apple provides to deliver what you bought.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The game contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking software. Nothing you do is tracked across other apps or websites, and no data is sold or shared with data brokers. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash and usage diagnostics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share those with developers, under Apple's own terms.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Names are filtered, and there is no chat or messaging.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
