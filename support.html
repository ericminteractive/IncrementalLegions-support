Common questions

I found a bug, or I have feedback or an idea.
Please email me. Feedback shapes what I work on next.

Will my progress carry over to a new device?
Yes, if you sign in with Apple in Settings: your progress is backed up to your account, and on the new device you sign in and choose to load it. Without signing in, your account stays on the device it was made on.

A purchase didn't arrive.
If a purchase is interrupted, the App Store hands it back to the game the next time you open it. If it still doesn't arrive, email me with your App Store receipt and I will make it right.

Why hasn't my place on the rankings changed?
Rankings update when you're online. The game never waits on the server, so you can keep playing offline, and your bests are sent once you're back online.

How do I report or block a player?
In the rankings, press and hold the player's row, then choose Report name or Block. Reports are read by a person.

How do I delete my account?
In Settings, choose "Delete account." It removes your account and everything under it from the server.
